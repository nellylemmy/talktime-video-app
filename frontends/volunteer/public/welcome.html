What is TalkTime?

TalkTime is a free video mentoring platform from the ADEA Foundation. It connects volunteers around the world with Maasai students in Rombo, Kenya for one-on-one English conversations — boosting fluency and confidence, one call at a time.

30 minutes

Each call runs 30 minutes — enough to connect, never a burden on your week.

Just conversation

No teaching degree, no lesson plans. Friendly talk is the whole job.

From anywhere

Secure browser video. All you need is a device, a connection, and a smile.

How it works

Three steps from invitation to your first conversation.

  1. 1

    Create your account

    Sign up as a volunteer in a couple of minutes. Choose how you want to help.

  2. 2

    Schedule a call

    Pick a student and book a time that suits you. You'll get reminders 30, 10, and 5 minutes before.

  3. 3

    Talk for 30 minutes

    Meet on a secure video call, chat, and watch a young person's confidence grow.

Two ways to volunteer

Whether you're a professional with time to give or a student earning service hours, there's a place for you.

Standard Volunteer

Help Maasai students practice English through conversation. Perfect for professionals, retirees, and anyone with time to spare and a heart to serve.

  • Flexible scheduling — book when it suits you
  • One-on-one calls with students
Enhanced benefits

Student Volunteer

Are you a student? Earn community service hours and connect with peers in Rombo — a meaningful way to give back while building cross-cultural friendships.

  • Certificates & official documentation

  • Parental approval required if under 18
