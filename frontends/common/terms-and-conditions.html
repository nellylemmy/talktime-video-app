1. Introduction

TalkTime is a video mentoring platform operated by ADEA Foundation, a non-profit organization registered in Kenya. The platform connects international volunteers with Maasai students in Kenya for English language practice through live video sessions.

By accessing or using TalkTime, you agree to be bound by these Terms and Conditions. If you do not agree with any part of these terms, you must not use the platform.

2. Eligibility

To use TalkTime as a volunteer, you must meet the following requirements:

  • You must be at least 18 years of age, or have verified parental or guardian approval if under 18.
  • You must be able to communicate in English at a conversational level.
  • You must have access to a device with a camera, microphone, and stable internet connection.

Students participate in TalkTime through school-based programs. Student access is managed through institutional consent provided by partnering schools, and parental or guardian approval is obtained as part of the enrollment process.

4. User Obligations

All users of TalkTime are expected to:

  • Report any concerns about student safety or inappropriate behavior through the platform's reporting mechanisms.

Violation of these rules may result in immediate account termination and, where applicable, referral to relevant authorities.

6. Video Mentoring Sessions

TalkTime video mentoring sessions operate under the following rules:

  • Each session has a standard duration of 30 minutes. Sessions automatically end when the time limit is reached.
  • Students are limited to one session per day.
  • A maximum of 3 active (scheduled) meetings may exist between the same volunteer-student pair at any given time.
  • Sessions cannot be scheduled more than 3 months in advance.
  • If a session is not joined within 30 minutes of its scheduled start time, it is automatically marked as missed.

Volunteers are expected to maintain reliable attendance. The following performance thresholds apply:

  • A cancellation rate of 40% or higher may result in account restriction.
  • A missed session rate of 30% or higher may result in account restriction.

These thresholds exist to protect students from unreliable scheduling and to maintain the quality of the mentoring experience.

7. Data Handling

ADEA Foundation collects and processes the following data to operate TalkTime:

  • Personal information provided during registration (name, email address, country, and profile details).
  • Session logs including scheduled times, attendance records, and session durations.
  • Technical data such as browser type, device information, and IP addresses for security and troubleshooting purposes.

Authentication is handled using JSON Web Tokens (JWT). No session cookies are stored on your device for authentication purposes.

ADEA Foundation does not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Data is retained for as long as your account remains active. Upon account deletion, personal data is removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

8. Child Protection

TalkTime serves minors. The safety of students is our highest priority. The following safeguards are in place:

  • Parental or guardian approval is required for all student participants under 18 years of age.
  • Session duration is limited to 30 minutes with automatic termination.
  • All volunteers undergo a review process before being granted access to schedule sessions with students.
  • The platform provides reporting mechanisms for any user to flag concerning behavior.
  • ADEA Foundation reserves the right to immediately suspend any account and review session records if a child safety concern is raised.

Any user who becomes aware of behavior that may endanger a child is obligated to report it immediately through the platform or by contacting ADEA Foundation directly.
